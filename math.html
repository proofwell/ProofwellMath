    Getting started
    
    
    How do I answer a problem?
    Type your answer in the box and press Enter or Check. You can type whole numbers (12), negatives (-4), decimals (2.5) or fractions (3/4). For equations you can type just the number or x = 5.
    
    
    Do I need to simplify fractions?
    No. 2/4 and 1/2 are both counted as correct. Decimals that equal the fraction work too.
    
    
    What happens if I get it wrong?
    You get a second try. If the second try is also wrong, we show the answer and move on. Your streak resets, but you never lose XP.
    
    
    XP & levels
    
    
    How much XP do I get?
    Harder skills give more: +1 on levels 1–5, +2 on 6–10, +3 on 11–15 and +4 on 16–20. Streaks add +1 at 5 in a row and +2 at 10 in a row. A right answer on the second try is +1. You also get +1 XP per minute of practice.
    
    
    What do levels unlock?
    Every 20 XP is a new level, and each level unlocks the next skill on your path. Once you've passed all 20 skills you get mixed challenges at full difficulty.
    
    
    Does skipping cost XP?
    No, but it resets your streak.
    
    
    Why is my XP saving slowly?
    To keep things fair, XP is saved at most once every couple of seconds. If you answer quickly, it catches up on its own — don't close the tab right away.
    
    
    Account
    
    
    Can I practice without an account?
    Yes — choose “Continue as guest” on the sign-in page. Guest XP is only kept in this browser.
    
    
    I forgot my password.
    On the sign-in page, choose “Forgot password?” and we'll email you a reset link.
    
    
    Does Proofwell work on Chromebooks?
    Yes. It works in any modern browser on Chromebooks, Windows, Mac, iPad and Android.
